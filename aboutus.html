Who we are

About FFVERSE

Free tools built to help Free Fire players enjoy the game and play at their best.

Our story

FFVERSE is an independent project made for Free Fire and Free Fire MAX players. Every player holds their phone differently, and every phone responds differently. Finding settings that feel right can take a lot of trial and error. We started FFVERSE to make that easier, with simple tools that anyone can use for free.

What we do

Our main tool is the sensitivity generator. You choose your device and how you play, and it gives you a set of sensitivity values to try, which you can save as presets on your own device. We also plan to keep improving the site and adding new tools for players over time.

What we stand for

Free for everyone

Our tools are free to use. There is no paywall and you don't need to pay to get the best of the site.

Made for phones

Most players play on mobile, so we design every page to be fast and easy to use on a small screen.

Respect for your privacy

We only collect what we need to run the site. Read our Privacy Policy to see exactly how.

Built with your feedback

Players tell us what works and what doesn't, and that shapes what we fix and build next.

Keeping FFVERSE running

Running a website costs money for hosting, a domain and regular updates. Donations are completely optional, but they help us keep the tools free and keep them working after game updates. If you want to support us, you can do that on our donate page.
