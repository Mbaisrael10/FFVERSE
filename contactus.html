Quick answers

How fast do you reply?

We usually respond within 24 to 48 hours. Busy periods like new game updates can take a little longer.

My sensitivity feels off. What should I send?

Select "Sensitivity generator help", add your phone model, and tell us what feels too fast or too slow. The more detail, the better we can help.
