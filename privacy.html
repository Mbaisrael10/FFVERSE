1. Who we are

FFVERSE is an independent website offering tools for Free Fire and Free Fire MAX players, such as a sensitivity generator. FFVERSE is a fan project. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by Garena or Free Fire.

For questions about your data, email us at support@ffverse.com.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, the topic, your phone model (optional) and the message you write.
  • Donations. Your email address, your name (optional), an optional message, the amount and a payment reference. Payments are handled by Paystack. We never see or store your full card number, PIN or other card details.
  • Accounts. If you create an account, we collect your email address, the name or profile details you provide, and the avatar you choose. If you sign in with Google or Facebook, we receive the basic profile details that service shares with us, such as your name and email address.

Information stored on your device

  • Sensitivity generator. Settings and saved presets are kept in your browser's local storage on your own device. They are not sent to us.
  • Contact form timer. A timestamp is kept in your browser so the form can limit how often messages are sent.

Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may record technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested and the time of the request. This is used to keep the site running, secure and free from abuse.

3. Cookies and local storage

We use your browser's local storage to remember your presets and to limit repeated form submissions. If you have an account, our sign-in provider also stores session information in your browser so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. If that changes, we will update this policy and, where required, ask for your consent first.

You can clear local storage and cookies at any time in your browser settings. Doing this will delete saved presets and sign you out.

4. How we use your information

  • To reply to your messages and give support.
  • To process donations, send receipts and say thank you.
  • To create and manage your account and keep it secure.
  • To run, maintain, fix and improve the website and our tools.
  • To prevent spam, fraud and abuse.
  • To meet our legal obligations.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on your consent (for example, when you submit a form or sign in with a social account), the need to carry out a request you make of us (such as processing a donation), our legitimate interest in running and protecting the site, and compliance with the law.

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site, and only as needed for them to do their job:

  • Paystack processes donations and card, bank transfer and USSD payments.
  • Supabase provides sign-in and database services for accounts.
  • Google and Facebook provide optional social sign-in.
  • Formspree (or a similar form service) delivers contact form messages to us by email.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts used on our pages, which means your browser contacts Google when a page loads.
  • Our hosting provider serves the website and keeps server logs.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy that governs how it handles your data. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to respond to valid legal requests, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users and the site.

6. Where your data is processed

Some of our providers store or process data on servers outside Nigeria. When that happens, we take reasonable steps to make sure your information is protected in line with applicable data protection law.

7. How long we keep it

  • Contact messages are kept for as long as needed to answer you and handle any follow-up.
  • Payment records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes.
  • Account information is kept until you ask us to delete your account.
  • Server logs are kept for a short period set by our hosting provider.

When we no longer need information, we delete it or make it anonymous.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and access controls on our account database. No website or method of transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Your rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023, and other laws that apply to you, you may have the right to:

  • Ask whether we hold personal information about you and get a copy of it.
  • Ask us to correct information that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Ask us to delete your information, where we have no legal reason to keep it.
  • Object to or ask us to restrict certain uses of your information.
  • Withdraw your consent at any time. This does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it.
  • Make a complaint to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission if you believe your rights were not respected.

To use any of these rights, email us at the address in section 13. We may ask you to confirm your identity before we act on a request, and we will reply as soon as we reasonably can.

10. Children

Many Free Fire players are young, so we take this seriously. FFVERSE is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before you create an account, send us a message or make a donation. If you believe a child has given us personal information without permission, email us and we will delete it.

11. Other websites

Our site may link to other websites. We are not responsible for their content or how they handle your information, so please read their privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. If a change is significant, we will make a clear notice on the site. Please check this page now and then.
